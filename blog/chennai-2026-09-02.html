Chennai: A City Felt, Not Just Seen

Chennai has a way of settling in your bones, a city people find themselves returning to, often for the smallest, most vivid details. It’s the unparalleled filter coffee, served with a flourish, its rich aroma blending with the heady scent of jasmine from temple garlands. You notice the genuine warmth in strangers’ eyes, the easy rhythms of daily life unfolding amidst the bustle.

It’s seeing a flash of brilliant silk against ancient temple architecture, or discovering a quiet, shaded courtyard where time seems to slow. And then there’s the distinct feeling of the humid air before the evening breeze kicks in, carrying the scent of salt and spices from the coastline, drawing you towards Marina Beach where the kites are just beginning to catch the wind...

Bengaluru: The Comfort in the Chaos

Bengaluru, for all its dynamic buzz, has a way of settling into your senses, leaving you with specific, quiet longings. Travelers often speak of the city’s mornings – the irresistible pull of a filter coffee, its deep, roasted aroma mingling with the faint, sweet fragrance of jasmine sold on street corners. There’s a particular warmth that seeps into you, whether it’s from the steam rising off a perfectly crisp dosa at a storied tiffin room, or the genuine smiles shared across crowded tables. It’s this blend of vibrant energy and comforting ritual that people recall, that feeling of being perfectly at home yet utterly captivated. You find yourself thinking about the dappled light under the old banyan trees, or the sound of the dosa batter sizzling gently as it spreads across the hot griddle...

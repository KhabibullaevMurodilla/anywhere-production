The Gentle Pull of Antalya

Antalya isn't just about azure waters and sun-drenched beaches; it's the quiet magic travellers discover tucked away. It’s the distinct scent of orange blossoms mingling with jasmine carried on the breeze through Kaleiçi’s labyrinthine alleys, a fragrance many mention wanting to bottle and take home. People talk about the specific quality of light that hits Hadrian’s Gate at sunset, turning ancient stone to gold, or the unexpected delight of fresh simit and strong tea by the historic Roman harbour as fishing boats bob gently.

What genuinely seems to captivate, and keeps drawing people back, is the feeling of losing track of time. Perhaps you're admiring a hand-painted ceramic tile in a hidden artisan's workshop, when the distant call to prayer drifts over terracotta rooftops, momentarily silencing the gentle clatter of teacups from a nearby cafe, and you wonder how many more secret gardens...

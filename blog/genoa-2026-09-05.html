Genoa: The Details That Linger

Genoa has a way of seeping into your memory, not with grand gestures, but through quiet, persistent details. It’s in the deliberate loss within the carrugi, where shadows lengthen and suddenly open onto a forgotten piazza. People often speak of the thrill of peering through an unassuming archway, finding themselves face-to-face with the faded frescoes of a Palazzo dei Rolli courtyard, or spotting a tiny, vibrant majolica tile amidst centuries of stone. The scent of sea air and fresh focaccia is constant, but it’s the light that truly defines it – especially the way the late afternoon sun spills down a narrow alley and illuminates a single, ornate iron balcony.

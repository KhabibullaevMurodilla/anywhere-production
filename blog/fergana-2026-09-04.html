The Luminous Blue of Fergana

Fergana often feels like the Uzbekistan you discover for yourself, a place that whispers rather than shouts. It's the vibrant hum of the silk workshops in Margilan, where threads shimmer with stories untold, or the quiet focus of a Rishtan ceramicist, painting patterns passed down through generations.

There’s a distinct, earthy scent to the bazaars here, mingling with the perfume of impossibly fresh apricots and grapes that taste like sunshine. People speak of the warm welcome, the genuine curiosity in local eyes. You might find yourself drawn into a conversation over endless cups of tea, watching the world go by, feeling a connection that runs deeper than a mere visit. Or maybe it’s simply the way the light falls on those incredible, luminous blue tiles...
